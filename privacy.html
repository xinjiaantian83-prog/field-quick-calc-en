Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

Field Quick Calc is a jobsite calculation helper app. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information.

Information We Collect

Field Quick Calc does not collect personal information. The app does not require an account, login, name, email address, phone number, or other personal details.

Location and Device Data

Field Quick Calc does not request or collect location information. The app does not collect device identifiers, contact data, photos, files, or other sensitive device data.

Network Transmission

Field Quick Calc is designed as a local calculation tool. Calculation entries and results are processed on the device and are not sent to an external server by the app.

Third-Party Services

Field Quick Calc does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, crash reporting services, or tracking tools.

Data Retention

Because Field Quick Calc does not collect personal information, it does not retain personal information on external systems.

Children's Privacy

Field Quick Calc is a general utility app and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when needed. Any changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.
