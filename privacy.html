Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Field Quick Calc is a jobsite calculation helper app. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information.

Information We Collect

Field Quick Calc does not require an account, login, name, email address, phone number, or other personal details.

Location and Device Data

Field Quick Calc does not request or collect location information. The app does not collect device identifiers, contact data, photos, files, or other sensitive device data.

Network Transmission

Field Quick Calc is designed as a local calculation tool. Calculation entries and results are processed on the device and are not sent to an external server by the app.

Advertising and Consent

Advertising and in-app purchases are currently disabled. If advertising is enabled in a future release, Field Quick Calc may use Google Mobile Ads and the User Messaging Platform. These services may process device and advertising-related information for ad delivery, consent management, and fraud prevention. A consent or privacy-options form will be provided where required.

In-App Purchases and Tips

If enabled, one-time purchases and optional tips are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. Field Quick Calc does not receive or store payment-card information. Tips are optional and do not unlock calculation features.

Data Retention

Calculation inputs and results remain on the device. Any information processed by an enabled store or advertising service is subject to that service's retention practices.

Children's Privacy

Field Quick Calc is a general utility app and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when needed. Any changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.
